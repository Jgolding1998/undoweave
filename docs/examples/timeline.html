About this snapshot

Journal totals are a separate status snapshot; shown history may be limited.

Only changes made through UndoWeave are attributed. Actor labels are self-reported. Observed rows are unattributed file-sampling evidence; they are never undo targets. “Undo recorded” means the journal links an undo to that edit; a journal status does not establish whether undo will succeed now. The normal undo flow still requires a fresh preview and your approval.

No source content, diffs, hashes or preview tokens are included. File paths and labels can still contain private information. Review this report before sharing it.
